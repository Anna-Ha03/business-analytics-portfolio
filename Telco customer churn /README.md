# Telco Customer Retention Analysis

## Business Objective

Customer churn is a major challenge for subscription-based businesses. This project aims to identify customers at high risk of leaving and provide insights to support targeted retention strategies.

## Tools

- Python
- Pandas
- Scikit-learn
- Machine Learning
- ANN
- Survival Analysis

## Project Overview

The project analysed customer subscription data to:
- Identify key factors associated with customer churn.
- Predict customers who are likely to discontinue their subscriptions.
- Determine the optimal timing for retention activities.

## Methodology

- Data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
- Machine Learning models for churn prediction.
- Survival analysis to understand customer retention patterns.

## Key Findings

- Identified 284 monthly subscribers who were at high risk of churn.
- Survival analysis showed that the first 12–15 months represent a critical retention window.
- Developed retention recommendations to improve targeting efficiency, with an estimated saving of approximately £7.8K per year.

## Business Recommendations

The analysis suggests prioritising early-stage retention campaigns for high-risk customers, allowing businesses to allocate resources more effectively and reduce customer loss.
