# Toystore Business Analysis

## Business Objective

Analyse global toy business performance across products, suppliers and regions to identify key revenue drivers and growth opportunities.

## Tools

- SAS Visual Analytics

## Analysis

The project analysed:
- Business Performance
- Product category performance

## Key Findings

- Built dashboard to analyse global toy business performance across products, suppliers and regions.
- Analysed product categories to prioritise Board Games vs 3D Puzzles for future growth.
- Identified strong business performance with 58% profit margin and key revenue drivers.
